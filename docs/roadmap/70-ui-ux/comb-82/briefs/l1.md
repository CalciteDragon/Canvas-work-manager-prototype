# L1 — lens audit: visual hierarchy and layout

Read `common.md` (§ 1–9; § 10 is for walkers), `inventory.md` and `decisions.md` in this folder
first. They override `AGENTS.md`'s start-up reading. You are a cold auditor: you know nothing of
earlier audits.

## Your scope

All 194 matrix cells in `apps/e2e/ux-output/37dd16e2303e7d8968905de74767710d6b02b85c/` (`index.json` lists them: six seeds, both themes, 1440/1024/375 as the
matrix assigns them, including the `/prototype/design`, `/prototype/state` and development-dialog
cells, which only the lenses cover), plus the runtime surfaces in its `comb/` folder. Work surface by
surface through `inventory.md`, opening each cell's first screen **and its scroll frames** at full
resolution (crop with your image viewer; never judge a thumbnail), and use `.aria.yml`,
`.layout.json` and `.axe.json` to confirm what a picture suggests. Compare the same surface across
both themes and all three widths. Cite cells by path in `evidence`.

## Your lens: visual hierarchy and layout

What the eye lands on first on each surface, and whether that is the most important thing there;
alignment (edges, baselines, column starts: measure with `.layout.json` boxes, not by eye alone);
density and rhythm (spacing that repeats or does not); grouping (what reads as belonging together);
how a surface reflows from 1440 to 1024 to 375 (what is hidden, cramped, truncated, wraps badly or
overflows); scroll frames (what sits below the first screen and whether it should). Long or dense
content (busy, overdue, nested seeds) and empty content (`empty` seed) both.
