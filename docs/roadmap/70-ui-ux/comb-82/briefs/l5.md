Read `common.md` (§ 1–9; § 10 is for walkers), `inventory.md` and `decisions.md` in this folder
first. They override `AGENTS.md`'s start-up reading. You are a cold auditor: you know nothing of
earlier audits.

## Your scope

All 194 matrix cells in `apps/e2e/ux-output/37dd16e2303e7d8968905de74767710d6b02b85c/` (`index.json` lists them: six seeds, both themes, 1440/1024/375 as the

## Your lens: accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA)

- **axe**, every cell and runtime surface: `violations` by rule and impact, and `incomplete` (axe marks
  `color-contrast` incomplete on most cells, because text sits on translucent, blurred surfaces it
  cannot resolve; that is why you measure contrast yourself). `axe-summary.json` gives the counts;
  open the per-cell files for nodes. File real violations (one finding per rule and pattern, cells
  listed), at the severity their effect on a user warrants.
- **Structure** from `.aria.yml`: landmarks, headings and their order, names of controls, live regions.
- **Focus visibility** (the `skip-link-focused` surface; in a browser, Tab through one dense page per
  theme). Whether every core task can be completed by keyboard alone is another auditor's pass; do not
  walk the tasks.
- **Target size at 375** (SC 2.5.8, 24×24 CSS px or spacing): from the 375 cells' `.layout.json`, list
  interactive elements smaller than that with their boxes.
- **Measured contrast, both themes** — your `contrastTable` (required, both themes). For each cell
  below, in light and dark at 1440 (the phone drawer at 375), measure: body text, muted/secondary text,
  control text (button and menu labels), link text, input placeholder, and text on any translucent
  surface (drawer, menu, toast, dialog). Method, in the browser: reproduce the cell (`seed`, `goto`,
  `emulateMedia`), read the text's computed `color`; for the backdrop, if no ancestor up to an opaque
  background is translucent or blurred, use the computed background; otherwise sample the pixels
  behind the text: make the text transparent (`el.style.color = 'transparent'`), take
  `page.screenshot({ clip: <its box> })`, decode it in the page (`new Image()` from base64,
  `OffscreenCanvas`, `getImageData`) and take the median colour, then restore the text. Compute the
  WCAG ratio yourself. Cells: `busy-week-dashboard`, `nested-projects-project`, `nested-projects-kitchen-task-drawer`,
  `nested-projects-kitchen-more`, `nested-projects-archive`, `overdue-chaos-project`,
  `agent-heavy-settings-agents`, `agent-heavy-prototype-design`, and `nested-projects-phone-menu` (375).
  Each row: `{ "element", "cell", "theme", "fg", "bg", "ratio" (number), "required" ("4.5:1" or "3:1"),
  "method" ("computed" | "sampled") }`, colours as `#rrggbb`. A failing pair is also a finding (the
  table is the measurement; the finding is the defect).

Your findings file adds `"contrastTable": [ … ]` beside `findings`. Without a browser you cannot fill
it; say so in your report.
