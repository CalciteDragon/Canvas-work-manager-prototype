# L4 — lens audit: states

Read `common.md` (§ 1–9; § 10 is for walkers), `inventory.md` and `decisions.md` in this folder
first. They override `AGENTS.md`'s start-up reading. You are a cold auditor: you know nothing of
earlier audits.

## Your scope

All 194 matrix cells in `apps/e2e/ux-output/37dd16e2303e7d8968905de74767710d6b02b85c/` (`index.json` lists them: six seeds, both themes, 1440/1024/375 as the

## Your lens: states

Empty, loading, error, refused, archived, overloaded (dense), partial and recovered: for each surface,
is each state designed, or accidental? The matrix holds `empty`, `loading`, `error`, `refused`,
`archived`, `collapsed`, `remove-feedback` and the dense seeds; `comb/` holds a section whose read
failed and then recovered, a row failure, saving then saved, and the archive cascade. Does each state
say what happened, what it means for the user's data, and what they can do next? Is it the same kind
of state looking the same on different surfaces? Does a failure stay where it happened? What does a
new user with nothing see? The development dialog's delay and failure controls (in a browser, on your
own host only) can induce loading and error states the captures do not hold.
