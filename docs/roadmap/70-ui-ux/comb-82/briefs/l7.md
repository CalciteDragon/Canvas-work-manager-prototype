## Your lens: information architecture

Navigation (the sidebar tree, the phone drawer, breadcrumbs, the edit-project menu, page settings,
settings), where things live, and how many places one concept appears or can be changed. Can a user
predict where to find a project's pages, its archive, an archived project, agent connections, their
overdue work? Are there two routes to one thing that behave differently, or one name for two places?
How deep does a nested project go, and does the user keep their bearings (`comb/tree-at-depth`)? What
do `/calendar`, `/search`, an unknown route and the `/prototype/*` pages tell a user about where they
are? Map the places first (a short list in your scratch folder), then file what does not fit.
