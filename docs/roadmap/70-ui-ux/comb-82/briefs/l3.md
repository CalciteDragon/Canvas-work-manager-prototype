Read `common.md` (§ 1–9; § 10 is for walkers), `inventory.md` and `decisions.md` in this folder
first. They override `AGENTS.md`'s start-up reading. You are a cold auditor: you know nothing of
earlier audits.

## Your scope

All 194 matrix cells in `apps/e2e/ux-output/37dd16e2303e7d8968905de74767710d6b02b85c/` (`index.json` lists them: six seeds, both themes, 1440/1024/375 as the
matrix assigns them, including the `/prototype/design`, `/prototype/state` and development-dialog
cells, which only the lenses cover), plus the runtime surfaces in its `comb/` folder. Work surface by

`.layout.json` and `.axe.json` to confirm what a picture suggests. Compare the same surface across
both themes and all three widths. Cite cells by path in `evidence`.

## Your lens: affordance and feedback

Can a user tell what is interactive, and what each control will do, before using it (from the
captures: shape, label, icon, cursor-independent cues; at 375 nothing can rely on hover)? Does every
action confirm, offer undo or explain itself: look at the runtime surfaces (toast with Undo, saving
then saved, row failure, recovered section, archive cascade confirmation) and at the states the matrix
holds (`remove-feedback`, `refused`, `archive-confirm`, `more`, `page-settings`, menus and dialogs)?
Do disabled or blocked controls say why? Does anything look clickable that is not?
