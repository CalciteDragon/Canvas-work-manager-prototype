Direction

Eight principles, each grounded in reproduced evidence. They hold for every alternative.

Three frosted-glass alternatives

The same Kitchen workspace drawn three ways. These are throwaway mockups, not product code. The readout under each mock is computed live: it composites each translucent surface over the worst colour that can sit beneath it (including busy text), then measures text contrast.

Motion study

Every transition uses only transform and opacity: 120 ms for feedback, 180 ms for lifts, 240 ms for reflow. Entering eases out and leaving eases in. With reduced motion everything is instant, and your system setting is respected by default. Use slow-motion to inspect.
